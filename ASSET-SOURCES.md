# Photography and assets

Typography: Cormorant Garamond (normal and italic) and DM Sans, downloaded from the official [Google Fonts repository](https://github.com/google/fonts). The unmodified variable font files are served locally. Their SIL Open Font Licences are included in `public/fonts`.

- `public/images/home.jpg`: Clay Banks, [modern living room overlooking garden](https://unsplash.com/photos/modern-living-room-with-large-windows-overlooking-garden-BOYGjCdPx9o). [Original image](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1774199616762-31d947dc7d35). Contextual interior in Accord, New York; not represented as Sim’s property or a UK client’s home.
- `public/images/legacy.jpg`: Jill Dimond, [rolling fields and a mature tree](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-grassy-field-with-a-lone-tree-in-the-distance-a4w6UHNKY3U). [Original image](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1725021374659-4c1f0b2e7860). Down Thomas, Devon.

Both source pages identify the photographs as free under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license), allowing commercial use and cropping. They are served locally to avoid third-party image requests. They contain no people and are not client case-study evidence.

The original typographic monogram uses the letter S and a period. Interface icons come from Lucide. Fonts are DM Sans and Cormorant Garamond through the framework’s font loader. No competitor logos, copy or photography were reused.

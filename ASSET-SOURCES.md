# Photography and assets

The revised visual direction follows the Instagram screenshots supplied by the client: black/navy, yellow/gold, condensed uppercase editorial typography and the Better Call Sim wordmark. Anton is sourced from the official Google Fonts repository; its licence is in `public/fonts/anton-OFL.txt`.

`public/brand/better-call-sim-logo.png` and `public/brand/simran-sahota.png` are cleaned, AI-assisted reconstructions from those supplied screenshots, prepared with the built-in imagegen tool. They are design-preview assets; original high-resolution logo and photography should replace them when supplied. No biographical credentials were inferred from the images.

Asset prompts: reproduce the supplied Better Call Sim wordmark and telephone symbol without other post text; extract the same Simran Sahota portrait with identity, navy jacket, white shirt and expression preserved; replace the generated checkerboard with a uniform black background while preserving the foreground. The social-post claims of savings are not reused as client evidence.

Typography: Cormorant Garamond (normal and italic) and DM Sans, downloaded from the official [Google Fonts repository](https://github.com/google/fonts). The unmodified variable font files are served locally. Their SIL Open Font Licences are included in `public/fonts`.

[Original image](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1774199616762-31d947dc7d35). Contextual interior in Accord, New York; not represented as Sim’s property or a UK client’s home.
- `public/images/legacy.jpg`: Jill Dimond, [rolling fields and a mature tree](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-grassy-field-with-a-lone-tree-in-the-distance-a4w6UHNKY3U). [Original image](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1725021374659-4c1f0b2e7860). Down Thomas, Devon.

Both source pages identify the photographs as free under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license), allowing commercial use and cropping. They are served locally to avoid third-party image requests. They contain no people and are not client case-study evidence.

The original typographic monogram uses the letter S and a period. Interface icons come from Lucide. Fonts are DM Sans and Cormorant Garamond through the framework’s font loader. No competitor logos, copy or photography were reused.

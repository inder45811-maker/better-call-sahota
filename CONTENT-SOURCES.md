# Service copy source notes

Prepared 8 September 2026. Original educational service copy; sources were used to check the underlying concepts and material limitations, not copied verbatim. No firm credentials, authorisation, history, providers, fees, savings or guarantees have been asserted. Product and legal suitability requires individual assessment.

## Estate planning

- Wills and family inheritance: [GOV.UK — Making a will](https://www.gov.uk/make-will). UK jurisdiction differences are expressly acknowledged.
- Trust structure and responsibilities: [GOV.UK — Trusts and taxes](https://www.gov.uk/trusts-taxes); [trust registration](https://www.gov.uk/trusts-taxes/registering-a-trust); [trusts and Inheritance Tax](https://www.gov.uk/trusts-taxes/trusts-and-inheritance-tax).
- LPAs: [GOV.UK — Make, register or end a lasting power of attorney](https://www.gov.uk/power-of-attorney) and [choosing an attorney](https://www.gov.uk/power-of-attorney/choose). The page concerns England and Wales, not a UK-wide legal instrument.
- Inheritance Tax: [GOV.UK — Gifts](https://www.gov.uk/inheritance-tax/gifts), [working out tax on gifts](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/work-out-inheritance-tax-due-on-gifts) and [passing on a home](https://www.gov.uk/inheritance-tax/passing-on-home). No numerical tax thresholds or particular relief claims are used in the copy.
- Bloodline protection: editorial synthesis of will and trust planning, presented as an objective rather than a named legal product or guaranteed shield. The trust sources above support structure and limits.
- Business succession: original planning prompts; [British Business Bank — How to value a business](https://www.british-business-bank.co.uk/business-guidance/guidance-articles/business-essentials/how-to-value-a-business) supplies valuation context. Ownership/insurance coordination also reflects the business protection sources below.
- Care planning: [NHS — Paying for your own care](https://www.nhs.uk/social-care-and-support/money-work-and-benefits/paying-for-your-own-care-self-funding/) and [GOV.UK — Care and support statutory guidance, including Annex E](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/care-act-statutory-guidance/care-and-support-statutory-guidance). England-specific examples are not represented as uniform UK funding rules.

## Financial advice

- Investments: [MoneyHelper — A beginner's guide to investing](https://www.moneyhelper.org.uk/en/savings/investing/investing-beginners-guide).
- Pensions: [MoneyHelper — Types of pension and how they work](https://www.moneyhelper.org.uk/en/pensions-and-retirement/pensions-basics/types-of-pension-and-how-they-work) and [GOV.UK — Understanding tax and your pension](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/understanding-tax-and-your-pension).
- ISAs: [GOV.UK — How ISAs work](https://www.gov.uk/individual-savings-accounts/how-isas-work).
- Investment bonds: [MoneyHelper — What are investment bonds?](https://www.moneyhelper.org.uk/en/savings/investing/what-are-investment-bonds). The copy expressly distinguishes tax deferral from tax-free income and insurance bonds from debt securities.
- Life assurance: [MoneyHelper — What is life insurance?](https://www.moneyhelper.org.uk/en/everyday-money/insurance/what-is-life-insurance).
- Income protection: [MoneyHelper — What is income protection insurance?](https://www.moneyhelper.org.uk/en/everyday-money/insurance/what-is-income-protection-insurance).
- Business protection: [Legal & General — Business protection questions](https://www.legalandgeneral.com/insurance/business-protection/contact-us/) and [Royal London — Business protection](https://adviser.royallondon.com/technical-central/protection-guidance/business-protection/). These primary insurer materials verify generic product concepts; their commercial claims, policy-specific terms and provider names are not carried into service copy or presented as Better Call Sim affiliations.

## Property finance

- Mortgages: [MoneyHelper — Mortgage affordability](https://www.moneyhelper.org.uk/en/homes/buying-a-home/how-much-can-you-afford-to-borrow-for-a-mortgage), [mortgage in principle](https://www.moneyhelper.org.uk/en/blog/buy-or-rent-a-home/what-happens-when-I-get-a-mortgage-in-principle) and [repayment options](https://www.moneyhelper.org.uk/en/homes/buying-a-home/mortgage-repayment-options).
- Remortgages: [MoneyHelper — Remortgaging](https://www.moneyhelper.org.uk/en/homes/buying-a-home/remortgaging-to-cut-costs).
- Buy-to-let: [MoneyHelper — Buy-to-let mortgages](https://www.moneyhelper.org.uk/en/blog/buy-or-rent-a-home/buy-to-let-mortgages-explained).
- Bridging: [FCA — Mortgage Rule Review feedback, FS25/6](https://www.fca.org.uk/publication/feedback/fs25-6.pdf), notably the need for a clear purpose and credible exit. The copy does not promise speed, approval or future refinance.
- Second charges: [MoneyHelper — Second charge mortgages](https://www.moneyhelper.org.uk/en/homes/buying-a-home/second-charge-or-second-mortgages) and [further advances](https://www.moneyhelper.org.uk/en/homes/buying-a-home/increasing-your-mortgage-getting-a-further-advance).
- Equity release: [MoneyHelper — What is equity release?](https://www.moneyhelper.org.uk/en/homes/buying-a-home/what-is-equity-release). The copy includes estate, benefits, interest and future-choice implications without assuming every product has a particular guarantee.
